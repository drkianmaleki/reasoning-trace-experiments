- Design definitions agreed 2026-09-14 (evening), formal text in labeling_scheme_chat1.md Section 0e:
  - a_T = the source trace's own final answer. P_m = P(answer = a_T | cut(B_m)), cut(B_m) = the prefix ending with the last sentence of block B_m; blocks numbered from 0 like the node subscripts; cut-0 = the empty prefix stays the baseline. Δ_m = P_m − P_{m−1} (Δ_0 against cut-0), signed, no absolute values.
  - Trivial block: Δ_m = 0 (trivial for the answer only; still active in the transition tree).
  - Stopping rule: resample cuts in order; at the first cut with P̂_m = 1 (all n continuations end in a_T) set M and stop; later blocks are trivial by assumption, not measurement. Binomial facts: at n = 25, P̂ = 1 bounds the true P only at 0.887 (one-sided 95%); 25/25 occurs with probability 0.28 at true P = 0.95 and 0.07 at 0.90. Consequence pending Kian's decision: no resampled transitions after M; the E-arm may stop at its first or second cut.
  - Unresolved continuations ("?") stay in the denominator of P̂ (frozen-run convention; makes the stop conservative). Confirmation pending.
  - Tk(x) = token count of x (block, node, cluster of blocks, chain of nodes) in the studied model's own tokenizer, from one tokenization of the whole trace, tokens assigned to blocks by character offset, spans partitioning the trace so Σ Tk(B_m) = Tk(trace). The unit of localization.
  - Stage one reports, all descriptive and without thresholds: P_m and Δ_m with intervals; the block with the largest Δ_m and its Tk next to Tk(trace); the gap between the largest and second-largest Δ_m; the plot of Δ_m against m. "Localized" and "diffuse" are readings, not verdicts.
  - Two stages: stage one at every block-end cut under the stopping rule is the registered run; stage two, optional, node-level cuts inside the single block with the largest Δ_m, decided after the stage-one plot, budget permitting, registered separately. Near-tie rule open.
  - Typed arrows replace the glyphs: -> "immediately followed by", --> "followed later by" (rendered → and ⇢; ⇝ retired). Inclusive reading (-> a special case of -->) drafted; Kian decides. History form P(next = Y | W --> X); Kian's order comparison P(next = Y | W --> X) vs P(next = Y | X --> W). Transitions at Level 1 by default. Pattern brackets << >> proposed for ⟨ ⟩.
  - Paper one = the instrument (typing, the two trees, the localization numbers). Intervention (prompt-level, decoding-time resampling on the typed detector, weight-level) = paper two, stated as next step only.
