## Literature positioning (searched 2026-09-12/13; verify again before pre-registration)

- Nearest instruments: Thought Anchors (2506.19143), Thought Branches (2510.27484, ICLR 2026; nudged reasoning = diffuse, cumulative influence; on-policy edits 10–100× stronger than hand-written), Kortukov et al. (2606.11172; resample after every step to locate behavioral commitment), Scalena et al. "Beyond the Commitment Boundary" (2606.13603; sharp single-step boundary found with early exit — the contrast case to our no-jump resampling result; likely instrument-dependent).
- Observational transition schemes: FSM paper (2510.22437), Schoenfeld episodes (2509.14662), state-aware dynamics (2509.00190), ReasonOps (2605.29192; judges at 70–76%), Psyche (2512.00729; 5×17 hierarchical, LLM annotator ~60% consistency), ReasoningFlow (2606.05402; α > 0.8, F1 0.865).
- Item ancestry to cite: MiP-Overthinking (missing premise → self-doubt loop), Pause or Fabricate (fabricate vs pause; inferential boundary awareness), QuestBench; altered-riddle benchmarks (Riddle Riddle 2606.27103, PHANTOM RECALL, Altered Riddles) all measure accuracy, not dynamics.
- The gap we aim at: typed blocks as the state space with transitions estimated by resampling, compared against corpus-counted matrices; and the behavioral graph versus the discourse graph (ReasoningFlow showed discourse ≠ mechanistic). Negative-result caution: 2604.18307 found intervention effects did not localize to step types; pre-register that null.
- Path 3 (shuffled prefixes) is open but the prior runs against primacy (2605.07307: line-level shuffling of complete chains changes accuracy by <0.5 pp) and shuffled text is off-policy.
