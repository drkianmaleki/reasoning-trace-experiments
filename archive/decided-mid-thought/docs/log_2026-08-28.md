## 08/29 — A2 hand-check, the pre-registered scoring revision (v2.1), and rescored numbers

### A2: blinded hand-check (pre-registered, log_2026-08-22 §5)
Seed 20260830, recorded before reading. 20 continuations drawn uniformly at random
from the 500 resampling records, presented shuffled under opaque labels (HC-01..20),
full text with middles elided; I wrote all 20 verdicts on paper before opening the
key. Agreement with the regex scoring: 19/20.

The one disagreement, HC-01 = rs0823_c004_cut069_008: the reply ends
`Answer: (C)` — I read C; the regex scored NONE because it required a bare letter
directly after the colon. Diagnosis of this record exposed three scorer defect
classes across the dataset:
1. Letters wrapped in parentheses/brackets/markdown (`Answer: (C)`,
   `Answer: **C**`) scored as NONE.
2. Sub-word matches: `Answer: Ball = $0.05` matched the B in "Ball". Audit showed
   EVERY recorded B (8 draws) and most D,E draws were artifacts of this class.
3. Answers given as option TEXT with no letter, e.g.
   `Answer: "The question is not well posed."` — I caught this class myself while
   validating the flip list (record rs0823_c004_cut064_023, which the draft
   revision had scored NONE and which plainly states option E).

### The one permitted regex revision (§5: "regex is revised once at most")
Adopted scorer v2.1, implemented in scripts/rescore_letters.py: take the LAST line
beginning "Answer:" in the scoring segment (reply if thinking closed, else full
text — unchanged logic); extract standalone uppercase letters A–F from that line
((?<![A-Za-z])[A-F](?![A-Za-z])); if none, match the line against the six option
texts, accepting a unique hit only.

Validation: every record whose scoring changes was reviewed by me by eye against
its text — 35 records total; 34 flips approved from the draft sheet, 1 objection
(cut064_023 → E) which motivated the option-text fallback; the three option-text
recoveries (cut000_015, cut058_017, cut064_023, all → E) validated individually.
Evidence committed: docs/rescore_validation_sheet_v21.pdf and
docs/rescore_change_list_v21.csv. Per §5 the revision is now final and results
stand.

trace"; it came from chat-era analysis with the method unrecorded. Recomputed

snippet, recorded here) and spot-checked five seeded traces by eye (seed 20260831,

("$1 more", "one dollar more", "a dollar more") also hits 194/194 and moves the
median by roughly one character (the $ sign), so no earlier-in-paraphrase effect
exists. Write-up wording is pinned accordingly: every trace EXPLICITLY MENTIONS
the famous constraint, early (median 7.6% of trace); whether the trace then
relies on it is a separate, unmeasured question. Recount snippet of record:
